## Common versus optional content

- **Observed shared shell:** desktop navigation, mobile menu at a narrow viewport, footer “Рубрики” and “Сторінки” headings with their links, social links, RSS email field/button, and legal footer text. HOME-009 checks the headings and named footer links on the homepage and `/architecture`; other routes are not exhaustively verified. Exact visibility/layout varies by viewport.
- **Observed catalog-specific:** breadcrumb, catalog heading, intro text, card list, location count, load-more action, and cross-links. Do not require the same controls or counts on every category until checked.
- **Observed place-detail-specific:** heading/type, optional contact/map/directions information, review area on the river example, and related blocks. Contacts and content sections can vary by place; missing optional fields are not automatically defects.
- **Observed editorial-specific:** article body/internal links/comments/share controls; the related-editorial region may have no cards in the captured page.
- **No mandatory section order has been established across the entire site.**

## Coverage gaps

1. The six main categories and 24 type subroutes from the sitemap have not all been checked individually. CAT-003–CAT-023 now have partial Chromium automation against `/nature`; CAT-005–CAT-007, CAT-011, CAT-016, and CAT-017 are skipped because marker/ad/sort requirements lack stable signals or confirmed rules. Counts beyond the live label, ad placement, sort semantics/data signals, viewport breakpoints, and sibling-category behavior remain unverified. The live sample does not establish all category-specific filters, sorting, or list behavior.
2. Individual location and collection slugs are not exhaustively crawled; sitemap samples are not proof of availability.
3. Search now has designed positive/no-results/input-boundary cases, but query execution and result matching remain blocked by browser input permission; matching rules are unconfirmed.
4. Nearby permission-denied, unavailable-location, and recovery scenarios are specified, but outcome behavior still needs a controlled permission/device context.
5. Login/register/reset negative cases are specified; submissions and successful login/logout, saved/visited transitions, and profile persistence still require an authorized test account/environment and cleanup path.
6. Review filter/sort outcome cases are specified but unexecuted; available options, semantics, and result data need confirmation.
7. Map/directions destination checking is specified but unexecuted. Galleries, sharing, comments, RSS and contact forms were not submitted or comprehensively activated.
8. Unknown-route handling now has a designed case but no confirmed expected status, redirect, or error-page behavior. `/pr` is intentionally excluded by the existing sitemap/robots note; other lower-priority legal pages remain inventoried but not individually sampled here.
